FAQ

Common questions.

What is a search batch?

A focused search for one niche and one market, such as plumbers in Austin.

What makes a lead qualified?

A prospect must have verified SEO evidence, enough evidence signals, a usable contact path, and meet score thresholds.

What happens if evidence is missing?

The prospect stays in Needs Review. RankForge does not make strong SEO claims without supporting evidence.

Do Needs Review leads use credits?

No. Needs Review prospects do not consume qualified lead credits.

Do filtered-out prospects use credits?

No. Filtered Out prospects do not consume qualified lead credits and do not receive detailed audit-style claims.

Does RankForge guarantee clients or rankings?

No. RankForge provides evidence-based prospect intelligence, not guaranteed clients, rankings, or revenue.

What does AI do?

AI may help translate verified crawl signals into plain-language explanations, but it does not invent issues or make the final decision.
