Evidence gate

Why not every lead becomes qualified.

RankForge is designed to avoid unsupported SEO claims.

Qualified

Evidence-backed opportunity

Qualified prospects are evidence-backed and may be exported if the user’s plan allows it.

  • Verified SEO evidence
  • 3+ evidence signals
  • Usable contact path
  • SEO need score threshold met
  • Commercial fit threshold met
  • Overall score threshold met
Needs Review

Manual review recommended

Needs Review prospects do not consume lead credits.

  • Partial evidence
  • Missing contact path
  • High score but not enough verified evidence
  • Manual review recommended
Rejected

Filtered out safely

Rejected prospects do not consume credits and do not receive detailed SEO audit claims.

  • Wrong niche
  • Wrong city
  • Directory, franchise, or junk
  • No usable website
  • Failed pre-filter

Scoring model

The system checks more than whether a business exists.

FAQ

Common questions.

What is a search batch?

A focused search for one niche and one market, such as plumbers in Austin.

What makes a lead qualified?

A prospect must have verified SEO evidence, enough evidence signals, a usable contact path, and meet score thresholds.

What happens if evidence is missing?

The prospect stays in Needs Review. RankForge does not make strong SEO claims without verified evidence.

Do Needs Review leads use credits?

No. Needs Review prospects do not consume qualified lead credits.

Do rejected prospects use credits?

No. Rejected prospects do not consume credits and do not receive detailed SEO audit claims.

Does RankForge guarantee clients or rankings?

No. RankForge provides evidence-based prospect intelligence, not guaranteed clients, rankings, or revenue.

What does AI do?

AI may help translate verified crawl signals into plain-language explanations, but it does not invent issues or make the final decision.
