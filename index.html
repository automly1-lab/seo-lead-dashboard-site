FAQ

Questions agencies ask before starting.

Short answers for the qualification model, credits, exports, and outreach safety.

Is this a generic lead scraper?

No. RankForge is a prospect qualification workspace for SEO agencies. It uses evidence, contact readiness, and opportunity fit before a lead is treated as qualified.

Do all leads consume credits?

No. Only qualified leads consume credits. Needs Review and Rejected prospects do not.

Can I export leads?

Yes. CSV export is available on paid plans for contact-ready, evidence-backed opportunities.

Does RankForge invent SEO issues?

No. AI can explain visible signals, but it should not create unsupported SEO claims when evidence is weak or missing.
