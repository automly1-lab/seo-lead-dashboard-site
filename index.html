The problem

Most lead lists do not tell you who is worth contacting.

SEO agencies do not need another spreadsheet of business names. They need a clear reason to reach out, backed by evidence they can trust.

01

Raw lists create wasted outreach

Business directories and scraped lists rarely explain which companies are worth your team’s time.

02

Unsupported SEO claims create risk

A high score is not enough. Outreach should be based on evidence your team can actually review.

03

Manual research slows outbound

Checking websites, contact paths, market fit, and outreach angles by hand does not scale across cities and niches.

RankForge is built to help agencies qualify opportunities, not just collect rows.

The quality rule behind every prospect

Claims require evidence.

RankForge does not mark a prospect qualified just because it looks promising. Each lead is routed through an evidence gate that checks crawl signals, contact readiness, and opportunity fit before it becomes a qualified opportunity.

High score without evidence stays in review.
Rejected prospects skip detailed audit claims.
AI explains signals; it does not invent them.

Qualification

Qualified

Ready for review and export

Ready for review and export on eligible plans.

  • Verified SEO evidence
  • 3+ evidence signals
  • Usable contact path

  • Credit counted
Needs Review

Held back safely

Held back to prevent unsupported outreach claims.

  • Partial or missing evidence
  • Contact incomplete
  • High score but evidence not verified
  • Manual review recommended
  • No credit counted
Filtered Out

Not a fit for qualified outreach

Filtered out without detailed SEO audit claims.

  • Wrong niche
  • Wrong city
  • Directory, franchise, or junk
  • No usable website
  • No credit counted

FAQ

Common questions.

What does RankForge do?

RankForge helps SEO agencies find local businesses, verify available SEO and contact signals, and classify prospects as Qualified, Needs Review, or Filtered Out.

What makes a lead qualified?

A qualified prospect needs verified evidence, enough supporting signals, a usable contact path, relevant market fit, and score thresholds.

What happens when evidence is missing?

The prospect stays in Needs Review or is filtered out. RankForge should not make strong SEO claims without supporting evidence.

Do review-needed leads use credits?

No. Needs Review prospects do not consume qualified lead credits.

Do filtered-out prospects use credits?

No. Filtered-out prospects do not consume qualified lead credits.

Can I export leads?

CSV export is available on Starter and Growth plans. Free users can preview results but cannot export CSV.

Does RankForge guarantee clients?

No. RankForge provides evidence-based prospect intelligence, not guaranteed clients, rankings, or revenue.

What does AI do?

AI may translate verified crawl signals into clearer explanations, but it does not invent SEO issues or make unsupported decisions.
