Qualification

Qualified means evidence-backed.

Every prospect is routed through a qualification gate before it becomes an opportunity.

Qualified

Ready for review and export

Evidence-backed opportunities for paid-plan export.

  • Verified SEO evidence
  • 3+ evidence signals
  • Usable contact path
  • Score thresholds met
  • Credit counted
Needs Review

Held back safely

Held back to prevent unsupported outreach claims.

  • Partial or missing evidence
  • Contact incomplete
  • High score but evidence not verified
  • Manual review recommended
  • No credit counted
Rejected

Filtered out

Filtered out without detailed SEO audit claims.

  • Wrong niche
  • Wrong city
  • Directory, franchise, or junk
  • No usable website
  • No credit counted
High score alone is not enough. Evidence and contact path matter.

FAQ

Common questions.

What does RankForge do?

RankForge helps SEO agencies find local businesses, verify SEO and contact signals, and classify prospects as Qualified, Needs Review, or Rejected.

What makes a lead qualified?

A prospect must have verified SEO evidence, enough evidence signals, a usable contact path, and pass score thresholds.

What happens when evidence is missing?

The prospect stays in Needs Review. RankForge does not make strong SEO claims without verified evidence.

Do review-needed leads use credits?

No. Needs Review prospects do not consume qualified lead credits.

Do rejected prospects use credits?

No. Rejected prospects do not consume credits and do not receive detailed SEO audit claims.

Can I export leads?

CSV export is available on Starter, Growth, and Admin plans. Free users can preview leads but cannot export CSV.

Does RankForge guarantee clients?

No. RankForge provides evidence-based prospect intelligence, not guaranteed clients, rankings, or revenue.

What does AI do?

AI may translate verified crawl signals into clearer explanations, but it does not invent SEO issues or make unsupported decisions.
