FAQ

Questions before you start.

Is RankForge a generic scraper?

No. It is a prospect qualification workspace built around evidence, contact readiness and decision states.

Does every lead use a credit?

No. Only qualified leads use credits. Needs Review and Rejected prospects use 0 credits.

Can I export leads?

CSV export is available on paid plans for contact ready opportunities.

Does AI invent SEO issues?

No. AI can explain visible signals, but unsupported claims should not be used.
