How to use opportunity types

Use this page as the strategic layer above individual leads. Prospect Queue shows who to review. Qualification shows why. Opportunities explains the agency offer angle.

Most commonLocal SEO visibility offerUse when city, service, location, or local trust signals are weak. Keep wording cautious and evidence-based.
High valueService-page expansion offerUse when the site does not clearly support high-intent service pages or location pages.
Safe modeManual review requiredUse when crawl evidence is partial. Do not export as qualified unless the gate confirms evidence and contact path.
Product rule: opportunities are not claims by themselves. They become outreach-ready only when the lead has verified evidence, adequate score, and a usable contact path.
