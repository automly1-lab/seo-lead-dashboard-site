FAQ

Common pricing questions.

What counts as a qualified lead?

A qualified lead must pass the qualification gate, including verified evidence, enough supporting signals, a usable contact path, relevant market fit, and score thresholds.

Do Needs Review leads use credits?

No. Needs Review prospects use 0 credits.

Do Rejected or Filtered Out leads use credits?

No. Rejected and Filtered Out prospects use 0 credits.

What is a search batch?

A search batch tests one niche and market, such as dentists in Chicago or roofers in Austin. Search batches do not directly consume lead credits.

Can I export CSV files?

CSV export is available on Starter, Growth, and Admin Unlimited. Free users cannot export CSV.
