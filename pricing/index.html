FAQ

Common pricing questions.

What counts as a qualified lead?

A qualified lead must pass the qualification gate, including verified SEO evidence, enough evidence signals, a usable contact path, and score thresholds.

Do review-needed leads use credits?

No. Needs Review prospects do not consume qualified lead credits.

Do rejected leads use credits?

No. Rejected prospects do not consume qualified lead credits.

What is a search batch?

A search batch tests one niche and market, such as dentists in Chicago or roofers in Austin.

Can I export CSV files?

CSV export is available on Starter, Growth, and Admin plans. Free users can preview leads but cannot export CSV.

When do paid features activate?

Paid access activates after checkout confirmation. Pending payments should not unlock paid plan limits.

Does RankForge guarantee clients or revenue?

No. RankForge provides evidence-based prospect intelligence, not guaranteed clients, rankings, or revenue.
