This policy is written for the current MVP version of RankForge by CrestlineOps. It is not a substitute for advice from a qualified attorney.

1. Who we are

CrestlineOps operates RankForge, an SEO lead intelligence product for SEO agencies and consultants.

RankForge helps users create lead searches, review potential SEO opportunities, inspect lead details, and export lead data for outreach workflows.

2. Information we collect

We may collect account information such as your email address, authentication user ID, session information, and basic workspace settings.

We also collect search inputs you submit, such as niche, business type, city, country, and lead search criteria.

When RankForge processes searches, it may collect publicly available business information such as company names, websites, domains, public phone numbers, public email addresses, business locations, website signals, SEO-related observations, and contact path evidence.

3. How we use information

  • To create and manage your account and workspace.
  • To run lead searches requested by you.
  • To organize, score, and display business lead data in your dashboard.
  • To provide exports and saved lists.
  • To improve lead quality, product reliability, and user experience.
  • To prevent abuse, debug issues, and maintain service security.

4. Public business data

RankForge is designed to work with business information that is publicly available on websites, search results, business profiles, contact pages, and related public sources.

We do not claim that every piece of business information is complete, current, or error-free.

5. Service providers

The MVP may use third-party infrastructure and services such as Supabase for authentication, n8n for workflow automation, Google Sheets for the current data layer, OpenAI for structured analysis, SerpAPI or similar providers for search data, and GitHub Pages for hosting.

6. Data sharing

We do not sell your account information.

We may share data with service providers only as needed to operate the product. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the service, users, or the public.

7. User responsibility

You are responsible for how you use exported lead data, including compliance with applicable email, telemarketing, privacy, anti-spam, and outreach laws in your target countries.

RankForge does not provide legal advice and does not guarantee that any outreach campaign is compliant or successful.

8. Data retention and security

We may retain account, search, lead, activity, and export data while your account is active or as needed to provide the service, improve the product, resolve disputes, comply with legal obligations, or maintain internal records.

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures for the MVP stage, including authentication and user-scoped data access.

No system is perfectly secure, and you should not submit sensitive personal data that is not needed to use the product.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the CrestlineOps team through the contact method provided on the website or your onboarding communications.
